# Terms of Service

**This document has not been written yet.**

It is a placeholder shipped with the repository so the page, the consent checkbox and the
acceptance record all work end to end. It is not an agreement, and nothing in it binds anyone.

Replace this file with terms written or reviewed by a lawyer who knows the jurisdiction you are
operating in, then delete the marker on the first line. Until that marker is gone, Tradeloom
refuses to boot in production, and the page below carries a visible warning.

Whoever writes it will want to cover at least:

- who is providing the service, and where they are established
- what the service does, and what it explicitly does not do — in particular, that Tradeloom is a
  record-keeping and analysis tool and **not** financial, investment or tax advice
- that past performance, backtested or journalled, does not indicate future results
- acceptable use, account suspension and termination
- ownership: the user's trade data is theirs, and what licence you need to operate the service
- payment terms, renewal, refunds and cancellation, if you charge
- warranties, limitation of liability, and indemnity
- how the terms themselves may change, and how users are told
- governing law and how disputes are resolved

Bump `VERSIONS["terms"]` in `backend/tradeloom/core/legal.py` whenever the text changes, so
existing users are asked to accept the new version rather than silently held to it.
